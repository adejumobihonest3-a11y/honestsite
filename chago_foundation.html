About Us

The CHAGO Foundation

A professional training and leadership development institution dedicated to character transformation, ethical leadership, and personal effectiveness.

Certification

Graduates receive a globally recognized Certificate in Character Development and Leadership.

Our Mission

To raise responsible leaders through character development and leadership training. We design programs that target the core of human behavior to forge lasting excellence.

Our Vision

To become a leading institution for character and leadership development in Africa and beyond, serving as a catalyst for societal transformation.

The Founder

Chaplain Dr Adejumobi Honest Bamidele

Doctorate in Spirithoutherapy & Psychotheology

Dr. Adejumobi is an advocate of Character Reformation with a profound passion for helping individuals, families, and organizations become better through his unique blend of chaplaincy, leadership development, and advocacy.

He serves as a catalyst for those seeking to align their values with moral excellence and integrity. As a distinguished leader, social reformer, and spiritual architect, he is deeply dedicated to the holistic transformation of society.

"Character is the bedrock of any lasting success... When we reform the person, we transform the world."
